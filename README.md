# Sentience · 感识

A bilingual (EN / 中文) systems-level platform on the evolution of perception, intelligence, and consciousness across 3.5 billion years of life — from chemoreception in prokaryotes to predictive processing in the cortex to artificial cognition in silicon. Built around the thesis that intelligence began with perception and consciousness may emerge gradually as organisms construct increasingly sophisticated internal models of the world and themselves.

The site is single-page and includes a six-stage phylogenetic perception timeline (chemoreception → photoreception → mechanoreception → image-forming vision → echolocation → multisensory integration); a six-card cells-to-cortex module on nervous-system architectures; an animated SVG predictive-processing schematic showing world → sensors → perception → world model → prediction → action with a prediction-error feedback loop and a hierarchical-prediction trace; a five-card prediction ladder; a four-card predator-prey arms-race module; a six-layer memory module; a six-card social-and-language module; a five-card self-emergence module covering mirror test, body schema, autobiographical memory, metacognition, and narrative self; a six-dial cognition simulator that maps configurations onto known archetypes (bacterium-grade, invertebrate baseline, vertebrate-cortex, great-ape band, human-cognition, frontier-AI); a six-card civilization-as-externalised-cognition module; a four-card synthetic-cognition module on AI; and a five-question Q&A panel that explicitly carries epistemic uncertainty about consciousness. Two epistemic-warning markers flag the simulator and the AI section as places where speculation begins.
